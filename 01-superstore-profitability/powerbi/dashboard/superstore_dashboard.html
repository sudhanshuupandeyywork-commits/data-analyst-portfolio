Recommendation

  • Cap discounts at 20%, starting with Binders, Machines and Tables.
  • Re-price or drop the two Cubify printers and the Lexmark laser printer.
  • Review Central’s discounting habits (24% average discount, 7.9% margin) before blaming product mix.
  • Estimated recovery: 125K to about 185K over four years. The low end is the more likely, so test it in one region first.

Page headlines describe the full dataset; the filters change the KPIs and charts. Sample Superstore is a fictional retailer. Discounts and losses go together, but this data cannot prove the discount caused every loss.
